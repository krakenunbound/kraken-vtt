# The players' guide

You need only a web browser: Chrome (or another Chromium browser, such as
Edge) on a desktop or laptop computer. Phones are not supported.

## Joining a table

1. Open the address your GM gave you, such as `http://203.0.113.5:47300`.
2. Type **your name**, and choose **your password**. The first time at a table
   you choose it; it keeps your seat and your characters yours. The browser
   remembers you next time.
3. If the GM set a **table password**, type it too.
4. Press **Take your seat**.

If you lose your connection, open the address again: you come back to the same
seat.

## Your character

- In **Characters**, press the **New character** button (a figure with a plus) to open the game's character wizard. It walks you
  through each step: for Descending Darkness, kin, class, background and
  abilities; for Apeira Astra, Origin, Paths and skills.
- Your **sheet** opens from the Characters list. Click a skill, save or attack
  on it to roll it.
- Drag items, spells and other links from the books the GM shares onto your
  sheet.

## Rolling

- Click anything on your sheet that can be rolled.
- Type `/r 1d20+2` in chat to roll any dice.
- The **modifier box** below the chat adds to your next roll.
- Rolls, with their results, show in chat for everyone (unless the GM rolls in
  secret).

## In combat

- When the GM starts combat, the **combat tracker** shows the turn order.
- On your turn, target a creature (click its token), then roll your attack and
  damage from your sheet or the tracker. Hits and damage apply by themselves.
- In Descending Darkness, when a friend is dying, the **Steady** button in the
  tracker tries to stop the bleeding.

## Ability cards

If you like spell cards on the table, turn on **Ability cards** in
**Settings**. A **Cards** button appears in the sidebar. It opens your
spells and weapons as cards, grouped by level.

- **Click a card** to turn it over: its full text, and **Cast** (or **Attack**
  and **Damage** for a weapon).
- **Drag a card onto a creature's token** to target it and cast or attack in
  one move.
- A spell card **tips over and goes grey** when you have no slot of its level
  or higher left. **Long rest** gives every slot back and stands the cards up.
- The spell you are concentrating on **glows**. Turn it over and press
  **End** to stop.
- **Fan** shows the cards as a hand; **Spread** lays them out in rows.
- The cards only show your sheet: spending a slot on the sheet tips the card,
  and the other way round. **Card effects** in Settings turns the movement
  off.

## On the map

You see the map the GM shares, through your token's eyes: walls block your
view, and darkness hides what your light does not reach. Drag your own token
to move it.

## Chat

Type to talk. `/` lists the commands, and `/help` shows all of them. Some
commands: `/r` rolls dice, `/w <name>` whispers to someone, `/ooc` speaks out
of character.

# The games that come with Kraken

Two original games ship with Kraken, so a table can play with nothing else
installed. Each has a players' book, a GM's book of creatures, and a first
adventure with story, encounters, maps (with walls and doors), handouts and
art. Make a campaign with the game as its ruleset, then make its books active
in **Modules**. D&D 5E comes with its rules too: the SRD 5.2.1 (below).

## D&D 5E: the SRD 5.2.1

The System Reference Document 5.2.1 as three books for a D&D 5E campaign,
made from the SRD's own PDF:

- **SRD 5.2.1 Player's Rules** (a players' book): every rules chapter as
  pages (Playing the Game, Character Creation, Classes with their subclasses
  and spell lists, Character Origins, Feats, Equipment, Spells, Rules
  Glossary, Gameplay Toolbox), and what the character wizard, the sheet and
  the tracker use: 12 classes with a subclass each and their features to
  level 20, 9 species (the Elf's lineages, the Tiefling's legacies, the
  Dragonborn's ancestors), 4 backgrounds, the feats, every weapon, armor,
  tool and piece of gear, and 339 spells. Spells that roll (attacks, saves
  with half damage, damage and healing that grow with the slot or the
  caster's level, Magic Missile's darts, Bless's d4) play on the Combat
  Tracker.
- **SRD 5.2.1 Creatures** (the GM's): all 330 monster and animal stat blocks,
  by creature type, as NPCs whose attacks roll on the tracker.
- **SRD 5.2.1 Magic Items** (the GM's): the Magic Items chapter and its 258
  items, for parcels and the party sheet.

There is no adventure and no art (the SRD has none). Your own 5E modules and
packs sit beside these books. The books carry the SRD's licence notice:
This work includes material from the System Reference Document 5.2.1 ("SRD
5.2.1") by Wizards of the Coast LLC, available at
https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd. The SRD 5.2.1 is licensed under the Creative
Commons Attribution 4.0 International License, available at
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.

## Descending Darkness (dark fantasy)

The d20 family: roll a d20, add a modifier, beat a number. What makes it its
own:

- **Fragile heroes.** Characters go to level 10, not 20. Health is lower, and
  at 0 Health a character is **dying and bleeds** 1 Health at the start of each
  of their turns until someone steadies them (**Steady** in the combat
  tracker: a Wisdom check against 12) or they die. There are no death saves.
- **Dread.** A track from 0 to 6 on the sheet. Horrors, the dark and the dead
  raise it; at 3 a character is Shaken, and worse follows.
- **Kin, classes, backgrounds.** Five kin (Human, Elf, Half-Elf, Dwarf,
  Gnome), each allowing some of the seven classes (Fighter, Cleric, Wizard,
  Thief, Paladin, Ranger, Bard), and eight backgrounds.
- **Standard array** 17, 14, 14, 13, 12, 8; Defense instead of AC.

**Books:** *Descending Darkness Player's Handbook* (rules, kin, classes, 36
spells, gear), *Descending Darkness Bestiary* (18 creatures, each with its
portrait and token).

**Adventure: *The Empty Watchtower*** (levels 1 to 2, one or two sessions).
The beacon on the ridge above Hollowmere has gone dark, and the dogs will not
stop howling. Ten story entries, six encounters, three maps (the yard, the
upper floor, the crypt) and three handouts. The way through is not only a
fight: Aldous's journal says how the Warden can be relieved of his watch.

## Apeira Astra (science fiction)

Its own 2d6 engine:

- **Tasks.** Roll 2d6 + skill + characteristic modifier against **8**. The
  margin is the **Effect**: it adds to damage and decides how well things go.
  Difficulty adds or takes away (Easy +2, Hard -2, and so on); a **Boon** rolls
  3d6 and keeps the best two, a **Bane** the worst two. Untrained is -3.
- **Six characteristics:** Brawn, Reflex, Grit, Intellect, Training, Presence.
  **Stamina** is Brawn + Reflex + Grit. **Armour** subtracts from damage.
  A character is **Down** at 0 Stamina and dead at minus their Grit.
- **A crew in minutes.** Choose an **Origin** (Coreborn, Spacer, Frontier,
  Undercity, Belter), a major **Path** (Fleet, Marine, Scout, Merchant,
  Engineer, Medic, Drifter: its signature skill and four picks) and a minor
  Path (two picks). No levels: experience buys skills and characteristics.

**Books:** *Apeira Astra Crew Handbook* (rules, Origins, Paths, gear, salvage
law), *Apeira Astra Threat Dossier* (10 threats, each with its portrait and
token).

**Adventure: *Salvage Rights*** (a new crew, two sessions). The freighter
*Kestrel's Due* is adrift in the Heron belt, and a rival salvager is racing
the crew to it. But someone aboard may still be alive, and that changes
everything. Ten story entries, four encounters, two deck maps with hatches,
and four handouts.

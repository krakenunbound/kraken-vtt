# Keyboard and mouse

Maelstrom is mostly driven by the mouse: click to use, drag to move or share, right-click for a menu. This page lists the keys and mouse moves on the map, the quick slots, and other shortcuts. The map's keys work while the map has the focus, not while you type in chat or a note.

## The map

| Key or move | What it does | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Drag a token | Move it. The distance shows, coloured by its speed. | Everyone (own tokens) |
| Arrow keys | Move the selected token a square. With nothing of yours selected, pan the map. | Everyone |
| Number pad 1-9 | Move the selected token, diagonals too (7, 9, 1, 3); pan with nothing selected. | Everyone |
| **+** and **-** | Zoom in and out about the middle of the view. | Everyone |
| Mouse wheel | Zoom about the pointer. | Everyone |
| **Shift+wheel** over a token | Turn it (its facing). | Everyone (own tokens) |
| **Alt+wheel** over a token | Raise or lower it a square's distance (elevation). | Everyone (own tokens) |
| **Ctrl+wheel** over a token | Make it bigger or smaller, about a tenth a notch (a quarter square to 50 squares). | GM |
| **Ctrl+Z** | Undo the selected token's last move (a player's own if none is selected). | Everyone |
| **Escape** | Let go of the selection, close a menu, end an area template. | Everyone |
| Right-drag | Measure. Everyone sees your ruler. | Everyone |
| Left click or **Space** while measuring | Drop a waypoint. | Everyone |
| **Backspace** while measuring | Take the last waypoint back. | Everyone |
| **Ctrl+click** a token or tracker row | Target it. | Everyone |
| Right-click a token | Its menu. | Everyone |
| **Shift+click** with the ping tool | Ping and pull every player's view to that spot. | GM |
| **Ctrl+A** (pointer over the map) | Select every token. | GM |
| **Ctrl+C** / **Ctrl+V** (pointer over the map) | Copy tokens; paste them at the pointer. Kept across scenes. | GM |
| **Delete** or **Backspace** | Remove the selected token. | GM |
| **H** | Hide or show the selected token. | GM |

### The map editor

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Enter** | Finish the shape you are drawing. |
| **Escape** | Drop the shape you are drawing. |
| **Delete** or **Backspace** | Delete the selected walls or text. |
| **Ctrl+Z** | Undo the last change to the map. |

## Quick slots

The twelve quick slots along the bottom keep things at hand: a book or a page, a map, a character, a sound, a note, dice, or a chat line.

- **Use one:** click it, or press its key, F1 to F12. A key whose slot is empty does what the browser does (F5 reloads, F11 is full screen).
- **Fill one:** drag anything onto it: a link, or any window by the anchor at the top left of its title bar (Cards, Notes, the party sheet...). Dice in your hand go on a slot with a click; a chat macro goes on by dragging the hand beside the chat's entry.
- **Its menu:** right-click a slot to use it, rename it, or **Clear this slot**. A slot is never emptied any other way.

## Dice

- Click a die in the tray to roll it.
- Right-click a die and pick how many to pick them up; click the table to throw them, the tower to roll them there, or a slot to keep them. A right-click or Escape puts them back.

## Chat

- `/` lists the commands; `/help` shows all of them.
- Text inputs that act on Enter wait while a Chinese, Japanese or Korean input method is composing, so your words are not cut short.

## Windows

- Drag a window's title bar to move it, and its edges to resize it.
- **Pop out** puts a window on its own, on another monitor. Its sidebar button closes it wherever it is.

## Not yet

The keys cannot be rebound yet.

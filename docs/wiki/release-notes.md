# Release notes

Maelstrom's releases, with their downloads, are on [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/krakenunbound/maelstrom-vtt/releases), and the full list of changes is in the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md). New builds reach the GM through the start screen's update (see [Installing and updates](installing-and-updates.md)). This page sums up the latest release and what came before it.

## 0.1.0-beta.1: The beta (latest)

Maelstrom leaves alpha. The highlights:

- **A full audit of the table:** 93 problems found and fixed, from backups and saving to what players can see, combat rules and Map Studio.
- **A library you can find your way around:** Modules and Books filed by kind and series, with covers; Assets by kind; Items, NPCs, Spells and Images by type or by book. See [Records, books and packs](records-books-and-packs.md).
- **Folders:** add books, sounds and art while the table keeps running, with no restart and no one disconnected.
- **The .kpack format, version 3**, documented for people and AI agents.
- **The party token and the party stash:** mark where the party is on a big map, and trade items through the party sheet. See [Character sheets and wizards](character-sheets-and-wizards.md#the-party-sheet).
- **Who holds a character:** the GM gives a character to a player, releases it for the players to look over and take, or keeps it.
- **Your settings follow your account:** UI scale, window places and the rest, from the app or any browser.
- **At the table:** whisper from a portrait, your own portrait (a picture or a WebM video), twelve quick slots on F1 to F12, Ctrl+wheel to size a token, and an NPC's picture shared with a right-click.
- **Local AI for the Assistant:** Ollama or LM Studio, on your computer or network, with no key.
- **Fantasy Grounds maps line up with their art**, every map pin comes through, and the character wizards no longer roll details or hand out gear on their own.

The full list is in the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md).

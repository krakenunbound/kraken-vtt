# Release notes

Maelstrom's releases, with their downloads, are on [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/krakenunbound/maelstrom-vtt/releases), and the full list of changes is in the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md). New builds reach the GM through the start screen's update (see [Installing and updates](installing-and-updates.md)). This page sums up what is coming in the next release.

## Coming next (unreleased)

### Rough edges smoothed

- A creature with a "players call it" name is called that on the players' combat tracker and in chat too.
- An identified item keeps its real name in tracker and chat lines, even when an unidentified copy of the same name is about.
- Drag a result from a random table's window into the chat's entry.
- In 5E, ADV and DIS from the modifier box tag a roll **[ADVANTAGE]** or **[DISADVANTAGE]**.
- Pause also holds a player finishing the character wizard or improving a character.
- A region switched off at the table stays off after Map Studio's Save to Maelstrom.
- Clicking a roll chip while typing in a note places the cursor instead of rolling.
- Smaller fixes to Fate dice, the Scene Aspects strip, the Forged Harm panel, Character Selection's Export menu and template labels.

### New at the table

- **Action links in text:** checks, saves and skills against a DC, damage, healing, XP awards and conditions become chips in books and notes; `/check`, `/skill`, `/save` and `/award` in chat. See [Targeting and effects](targeting-and-effects.md#action-links-in-text).
- **Scrolling combat text:** damage and healing float off the token.
- **Chat bubbles:** words spoken as a character or creature show over its token.
- **Range lines:** a creature's targets get an arrow with the distance, and long range or out of range.
- **Combat options:** roll initiative every round, stop at the start of each round, and creatures' hit points as listed, rolled or maximum.
- **5E Inspiration** on the sheet.
- **Map keys:** the GM's Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V for tokens, + and - to zoom, arrows to pan, the number pad for diagonals. See [Keyboard and mouse](keyboard-and-mouse.md).

### Bookkeeping

Arrows, potions, rations, torches and the load counted for you, each with a switch to hand-wave it, and short and long rests by each game's rules. See [Bookkeeping and rest](bookkeeping-and-rest.md).

### Dice, chat and combat

The modifier box and its keys for every roll, roll modes in the chat bar, links and inline rolls in chat, the party's group roll, combat options (initiative by kind, skipped turns, NPC numbering, Delay and Act now), Foundry's dice modifiers, and the Session Log. See [Dice and chat](dice-and-chat.md).

### Targeting, area spells and effects

Targeting from the map toolbar, area templates placed by spells, effects you can see on tokens, lights as effects, and damage cards. See [Targeting and effects](targeting-and-effects.md).

### The session

Automatic reconnect, internet play with UPnP, invite links with a QR code, pause, the Players window's controls (bell, view as, kick, ban), pull players to my view, and the campaign menu with backups and logs. See [Hosting and inviting](hosting-and-inviting.md) and [Campaigns and backups](campaigns-and-backups.md).

### The map at play

A ruler everyone sees, who sees health and names, fog of war kept by the host, senses and magical darkness, undo a move, locked movement that asks the GM, elevation, distance coloured by speed, traps and regions at the table, animated maps and sounds on a map. See [Maps and scenes](maps-and-scenes.md) and [Tokens, vision and lighting](tokens-vision-and-lighting.md).

### Records and the core UI

Every record can be edited, copied and deleted; one text editor; the campaign's own story; random tables as in Fantasy Grounds; sharing with one player; item identification; the campaign's own encounters, parcels and quests; Character Selection; and every game's sheet on one shared frame. See [Records, books and packs](records-books-and-packs.md).

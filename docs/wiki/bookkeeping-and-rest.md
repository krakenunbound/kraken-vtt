# Bookkeeping and rest

Kraken counts what the characters carry and spend, by each game's rules: arrows, potions, rations and water, torches and oil, and the weight of the pack. It also runs short and long rests. Every part has its own switch in **Settings > Bookkeeping**, so a relaxed table can hand-wave any of it.

## Settings > Bookkeeping

The GM's switches, each on or off for the table:

- **Ammunition**
- **Consumables**
- **Rations and water**
- **Light from the pack**
- **Encumbrance and bag space**
- **Variant encumbrance** (D&D 5E)

Switched off, that part is not counted and nothing is penalised.

## Supplies

The sheet's **Supplies**, on its Inventory tab, shows what a character carries and spends: the load, the containers, ammunition, consumables, rations and water, and light. The Castles & Crusades, Fate, Forged in the Dark and Worldbuilding sheets do not show Supplies yet, though rests work for them.

## Ammunition

- An attack with a bow, crossbow, sling or firearm spends an arrow, bolt, bullet or round from the character's stack (a full attack spends one a swing).
- With none left the attack is refused, and chat says why. Chat warns when a stack runs low.
- Traveller's guns count their magazine and load a spare when it runs dry.
- NPCs are never counted.
- **After a fight**, anyone who shot is offered **Recover** on their sheet for what can be picked up (half, in 5E).

## Consumables

Potions, scrolls, a wand's charges and a kit's uses are used from **Supplies**, or by dragging the item onto a creature (a token, a tracker row, a party row). A healing potion heals whoever it lands on.

## Rations and water

Each day that ends on the calendar, and each long rest, every party member eats a ration and drinks a day's water, never twice for one day. **Refill** fills the waterskins at a stream. Going without brings the game's penalty:

| Game | Going without |
|---|---|
| D&D 5E | Exhaustion (from the fifth day without food, the first without water). |
| D&D 3.5E | A Constitution check or nonlethal damage. |
| Basic Fantasy | Hit points. |
| Cairn | Deprived, then Fatigue. |
| AD&D 2E, Castles & Crusades | Left to the GM; chat says so. |

## Light

Lighting a torch (from the Effects window, or **Light a torch** in Supplies) takes a torch from the pack; a lantern burns a flask of oil. A player with none cannot light it. The GM's own drop lights it anyway, and chat says so. A burnt-out lantern is lit again by hand with another flask.

## Encumbrance and bag space

Supplies shows the load as a meter, by each game's rules:

| Game | The load |
|---|---|
| D&D 5E | Strength x 15; the variant encumbrance is its own switch. |
| D&D 3.5E | Light, medium and heavy loads. |
| AD&D 2E | Strength's weight allowance. |
| Basic Fantasy, Castles & Crusades | Their own numbers (C&C keeps SiegeCore's own encumbrance setting). |
| Cairn | Slots. |

- Each container shows its capacity: a backpack, or a bag of holding whose contents weigh nothing.
- **Put away…** moves an item into a container.
- **Unpacked:** an Explorer's Pack (or any pack whose text lists its contents) unpacks into its torches, rations, oil and the rest.
- **The penalty** goes on the creature as an effect. To-hit and save penalties (2E's -1 to hit, 5E Exhaustion) count on the tracker's rolls. Speed and check penalties are shown on the sheet and the effect, for you to apply.

## Rest

- **On the sheet:** **Short rest** and **Long rest** (in 5E, choose how many Hit Point Dice to spend).
- **For everyone:** the party sheet's **Rest the party**, and the tracker's rest buttons after a fight. The clock moves on.
- **The Cards window** has **Long rest** too.

Rests follow each game's rules:

| Game | What a rest does |
|---|---|
| D&D 5E | Hit Point Dice, hit points, spell slots, death saves and Exhaustion. |
| D&D 3.5E, AD&D 2E, Basic Fantasy, Castles & Crusades | Natural healing. |
| Cairn | Its own rest and sleep. |

With **Rations and water** on, a long rest also eats a ration.

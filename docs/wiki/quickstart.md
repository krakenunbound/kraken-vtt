# Quickstart

This page gets a GM from a fresh install to the first fight in ten steps, and a player from a link to a seat in four. It uses *Descending Darkness*, one of Maelstrom's own games, because it needs nothing else installed: its books, its adventure *The Empty Watchtower* and its maps all come with Maelstrom.

## For the GM, in 10 steps

1. **Install Maelstrom.** Download it from [the Releases page](https://github.com/krakenunbound/maelstrom-vtt/releases): `Kraken.VTT.Setup.exe` on Windows, the `.AppImage` or `.deb` on Linux. Details are in [Installing and updates](installing-and-updates.md).
2. **Make a campaign.** On the start screen choose **My Campaigns**, then **New campaign**. Give it a name and choose **Descending Darkness** as its game (ruleset).
3. **Host it.** Select the campaign. Type **Your name** (what players see you called). Leave **Port** at 47300. Set a **Password** if you want one, or leave it blank for an open table. Under **Players connect over**, choose **LAN** for players on your home network or **Internet** for players elsewhere. Press **Play**.
4. **Make the books active.** In the sidebar on the right, open **Library > Modules** and make the game's books and its adventure active. This brings their records (NPCs, items, encounters, maps, story) into the campaign. A book that is not active stays off limits.
5. **Invite your players.** Open the **Players** window (in the tools along the top of the sidebar). Its **Invite** is a link with the table password already in it. Press **Copy** and paste it in your group's chat. *Addresses to give your players* lists the plain addresses too.
6. **Let them make characters.** Each player opens **Characters** and presses **New character** to run the game's character wizard. You can make one for them too.
7. **Open a map.** Open **Library**, then the adventure's **Maps** tab, and click a map to look at it. It opens in its own window, which is yours alone (**Not shared**) until you choose **Share**.
8. **Share it and place tokens.** Press **Share**: the map opens on every player's screen. Drag characters and NPCs onto it to make their tokens. Players see only what their own tokens can see.
9. **Start a fight.** Open **Campaign > Encounters**, pick one of the adventure's encounters and choose **Place on this map** and **Add to tracker**, or drag creatures straight into the **combat tracker**.
10. **Run it.** On the tracker press **Roll initiative**, then step through the turns. Players target a creature by clicking its token and roll from their sheets; hits and damage apply by themselves. When the fight ends, **End encounter** gives its XP to the party sheet.

That is a whole session's loop. Next, read [Maps and scenes](maps-and-scenes.md) and [Combat tracker](combat-tracker.md).

## For players, in 4 steps

You need Chrome, Edge, Brave or Firefox on a desktop or laptop computer (Safari is untested), or Maelstrom itself on a Windows or Linux PC. Phones are not supported.

1. **Open the link** your GM sent you, or the address, such as `http://203.0.113.5:47300`.
2. **Type your name and choose your password.** The first time at a table you choose it. It keeps your seat and your characters yours, and the browser remembers you next time. If the GM gave you a plain address and the table has a password, type that too.
3. **Press Take your seat.**
4. **Make a character.** Open **Characters**, press **New character** (a figure with a plus) and follow the wizard.

More in [Joining a table](players-joining.md).

## Try it alone first

You can open a browser on the same PC, go to your table's network address from *Addresses to give your players*, and join your own table as a test player. It is a good way to see exactly what your players will see. The **eye** in a map window's corner (player vision preview) shows the same thing without a second window.

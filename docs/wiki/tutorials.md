# Tutorials

Short walkthroughs for the things a GM does every session. Each one takes a few minutes. Start with the [Quickstart](quickstart.md) if you have not run a game in Maelstrom yet.

## 1. Reveal the monsters when the party walks in

Players should not see the kobolds before they open the door.

1. Drag the creatures into the **combat tracker** (or place an encounter with **Add to tracker**). Press the **eye** on each row to hide it. Its token on the map hides with it.
2. Describe the room. When the party steps in, press the eye again: the creature appears in the tracker and its token appears on every player's map.
3. Press **Roll all** (or **Roll NPC init**) and then **Start combat**.

## 2. Run a fight from start to finish

1. **Start combat**. Everyone sees whose turn it is: the portrait of whoever acts appears at the top of the screen, and the round as each one begins. A player whose turn it is gets a banner across the screen.
2. Step through with **Next turn**. Drag the turn arrow (or click a row) to give the turn to anyone. **Back** undoes the last turn change.
3. Made a slip with the round? Type the right number into the round box at the top of the tracker.
4. Click a creature's side (Foe, Neutral, Friend, No side) to change it. A charmed guard switches sides in one click.
5. When it is over: **Delete foes** (or **Delete non-friendly**) clears the dead, **Clear effects** takes every effect off, and **End combat** ends it. Each asks once and has a **Cancel**.

## 3. Tell a player their target is out of range

1. The player clicks a creature's token to target it.
2. An arrow in the player's colour runs from their token to the target, with the distance on it.
3. When their weapon or spell has a range, the line says **long range** or **out of range**. "Your bow reaches 120 feet; that orc is 150 feet away."

## 4. Speak in a language only some characters know

1. Under the chat entry, choose a language from the **Language** menu (players see only the languages their character knows).
2. Type and send. Characters who know the language read your words; everyone else sees only its sounds.
3. As the GM you see the words and an **Understood by:** line naming who understood.
4. Add your setting's own languages (Thieves' Cant, Old Imperial) in **Settings > Chat languages**.

## 5. Give out treasure and experience

1. Open **Campaign > Parcels** and make a parcel, or use one of the adventure's. Drop it on the **Party** sheet's **Inventory** tab.
2. **Distribute** splits the coins and hands out the items.
3. On the party sheet's **XP** tab, drop the encounters and quests the party finished, then press **Award**. Experience already given is never given twice.

## 6. Ask the whole party for a saving throw

1. In a book, a story or a chat line, "DC 15 Dexterity saving throw" is a button. Click it.
2. Every player gets a card asking for that save and rolls it with their own modifiers. A summary lands in chat when they are done.
3. Or type it: `/save dex 15`, `/check str 12`, `/skill perception 13`.

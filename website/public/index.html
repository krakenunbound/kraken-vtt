Maelstrom VTT

A free virtual tabletop. The GM runs Maelstrom on their own PC and hosts the table. Players join from their browser, with nothing to install, or from the Maelstrom app.

Everything the table needs

Host from your PC

Create a campaign, press Play and give your players the address. They see only what you let them see: hidden tokens, GM notes and secret doors never reach them.

Ten games included

Each with its rules, a character wizard, its sheet, its books and a first adventure with maps. Nothing else to buy or install.

Maps that play

Square and hex grids, walls and doors, line of sight and fog, lights and darkness, weather, drawing, and animated tokens.

Map Studio

A built-in map maker: paint from art packs, draw walls and doors with art, add lights, weather, traps and regions, then play-test.

Sheets, combat and dice

Character sheets and wizards, a combat tracker that rolls and applies attacks, damage and saves, effects, treasure parcels and 3D dice.

Your Fantasy Grounds library

Own Fantasy Grounds modules? Maelstrom reads them in place: books, story, encounters, NPCs, items, and maps with their walls and lights.

Sound and atmosphere

One-shots, chat-triggered effects, ambience and music, each with its own volume for every player.

AI-first

Every GM action is also a tool for an AI agent, so an assistant can build characters, run combat or set up a map.

Games you can play today

  • Descending DarknessMaelstrom's own dark fantasy on the d20: fragile heroes, light that runs out, dread that builds.
  • Apeira AstraMaelstrom's own science fiction on 2d6: crews made in minutes and armour that soaks.
  • D&D 5E (SRD 5.2.1)12 classes to level 20, 339 spells, 330 creatures, 258 magic items.
  • Basic Fantasy RPGOld-school play: 3d6 in order, five saving throws, turning the undead.
  • CairnSaves under a d20, attacks that always hit, Scars and ten inventory slots.
  • Ironsworn and StarforgedVows, moves, momentum and oracles, with or without a GM.
  • Fate Core and AcceleratedFate dice on the ladder, aspects and fate points.
  • Forged in the Darkd6 pools, position and effect, stress and clocks everyone sees.

With your own books it also plays Castles & Crusades, D&D 3.5E, AD&D 2E, Traveller and a rules-free Worldbuilding mode for any system.

See it at the table

Download

Free to use. Maelstrom updates itself from its start screen.

WindowsInstaller (.exe) LinuxAppImage, any x64 distribution Debian / Ubuntu.deb package

The Windows installer isn't code-signed yet: if SmartScreen stops it, choose More info, then Run anyway.

  1. Make a campaign. Choose My Campaigns, name it and pick its game.
  2. Host. Choose LAN, Internet or both, set a password if you like, and press Play.
  3. Players join. They open the address in their browser, or join from the Maelstrom app, type their name and make a character.
